## WP3 (API surface and 0.3.0; submodule commit in this entry's commit, superproject pointer and archive moves follow)

Removed exports, all named in NEWS: `tmle_candidate`, `select_tmle_candidate`, `run_plasmode_feasibility`, `run_plasmode_dq_stress`, `run_negative_control_ladder` (internal engines, no shims).
